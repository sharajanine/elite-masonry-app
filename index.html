💡 Tips & Workflow

📸 Image Upload (Fastest)

Take a screenshot of vendor quotes. The AI will extract the address, vendor name, and price automatically. Works best with clear, readable text.

📝 Paste Text

Copy & paste emails, text messages, or quotes directly. Example: "ABC Masonry quoted $2500 for the driveway at 464 Ontario Dr"

🎤 Voice Recording

Speak the vendor quote naturally. Works on mobile (Chrome, Safari). The AI transcribes and extracts automatically.

✋ Manual Entry

Type in the form directly. Good as a fallback or if you prefer full control.

📊 Workflow

  1. Add jobs via image/text/voice
  2. Upload vendor quotes to compare prices
  3. Track payments with Net 30 Tracking
  4. Export data for your accountant
