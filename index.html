📸 How to capture better quotes for automatic extraction

⚡ Fastest way: Speak, paste, or screenshot - the app extracts vendor name + price automatically. No typing needed.

🎤 Voice capture (fastest on phone)

Just speak naturally! Press "Start recording" and say the vendor + price. Tap Stop when done.

Examples:
✓ "Bob from ABC Masonry quoted $2500 for the driveway"
✓ "Can do the job for $2851"
✓ "John's concrete, $3200 for patio at 464 Ontario"

Why this is best: Fastest on mobile, hands-free, works while you're on the phone with the vendor.

📝 Paste raw text (for saved messages)

Already have the quote as text? Just paste it:

Examples that work:
✓ "ABC Masonry quoted $2500 for the driveway at 464 Ontario"
✓ "Hi can do job for $2851"
✓ Pasted email with vendor name and price
✓ Text message from contractor

Why this is best: Works on any device, no microphone needed, copy/paste is instant.

Capture the vendor name clearly: ABC Masonry, John's Concrete, Pro Contractors - whatever it says, make sure it's readable in the photo/screenshot
Include the total price prominently: Make sure the dollar amount is visible and clear - $2,500 or 2500 or "Total: $2,851"
Show the project/job address if available: "464 Ontario Dr" or "Smith Residence" - helps group quotes together
Use text screenshots when possible: Screenshot text messages, PDFs, or emails - these extract more reliably than photos
Good lighting for photos: If taking a photo of a paper quote, use good lighting and shoot straight-on (not at an angle)
Crop to the important part: Focus on the vendor name, price, and address - don't include unrelated stuff

✗ DON'T: What makes extraction harder

Blurry or low-quality photos: Use your phone's "Photo" app, not old pics. Make sure text is readable.
Angled/skewed photos: Shoot straight-on. Angled photos are harder to read.
Multiple prices on one quote: If there's a discount and a total, make sure the TOTAL price is what gets extracted (we'll grab the biggest number we find)
No vendor name at all: Just a price with no company name? Upload it anyway - we'll label it "Unknown vendor" and you can edit later
Handwritten with messy writing: Photos of handwritten quotes are harder - type it in manually or take a clear photo if possible

⚡ Fastest workflow

BEST: Use voice (on phone) — Tap record → Speak quote → Tap Stop → Extract
Quick: Paste text — Copy text → Paste → Extract → Done
Formal: Upload image — Upload PDF or screenshot → Extract → Fix details if needed
Manual: Type it — Fill in address, vendor, price → Create

✓ Voice tips for better extraction

Speak clearly: Say the vendor name, price, and address slowly and distinctly (same way you'd spell it over the phone)
Use natural language: "ABC Masonry quoted $2500" works better than "vendor ABC Masonry price $2500"
Say the dollar amount clearly: "Twenty-five hundred dollars" or "twenty five hundred" both work
Quiet environment helps: Voice capture works best with minimal background noise
Can edit anything after: Price extracted as $2400 instead of $2500? Just click Edit and fix it in 2 seconds

If you have formal quotes as PDFs or already have screenshots, you can upload those too.

Capture the vendor name clearly: ABC Masonry, John's Concrete, Pro Contractors - whatever it says, make sure it's readable in the photo/screenshot
Include the total price prominently: Make sure the dollar amount is visible and clear - $2,500 or 2500 or "Total: $2,851"
Good lighting for photos: If taking a photo of a paper quote, use good lighting and shoot straight-on (not at an angle)
Crop to the important part: Focus on the vendor name, price, and address - don't include unrelated stuff

💡 Pro tip: Prefer pasting text whenever possible - it's faster, no screenshots needed, and works great on phones. Only screenshot if you have a formal PDF quote or prefer visual confirmation.
